Public data sources

HouseLens identifies homes from free public property records published by counties and states. Your phone requests small map areas (about 250 meters across) directly from these services; HouseLens's servers aren't involved. County sales files for a few counties are served from HouseLens's own storage, named by county only. 1,021 counties are covered as of 2026-10-14.
